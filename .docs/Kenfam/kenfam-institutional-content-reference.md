## 3. Publication Workflow

An editor records source URL, retrieval date, factual excerpt/paraphrase,
reviewer and approval date. Institution details seed/fallback data uses approved
facts only. Marketing copy is separately reviewed. Contact links normalize
telephone numbers to E.164; enable WhatsApp only after confirming the number.

About page: approved history, company purpose, values and team/office information.
Contact page: confirmed office/email/phone/hours, accessible map and inquiry.
Footer/mail/PDF: legal/display name, monitored contacts and approved address.
Do not duplicate values inside generic TravelTours templates.

## 4. Policy And Privacy Copy

Booking/cancellation/refund, child travel, identity-document handling, marketing
consent and privacy copy are new documents requiring client/legal approval.
The module stores policy version and acceptance evidence; engineering defaults
must not be presented as binding commercial or legal policy.
